Print for the wall

Colour goes in the text and labels only, so it is light on ink.
The finished writing, or the plan as a graphic organiser
Each sentence type in its own colour, matching the board
A small tag such as SD1 in front of each sentence
Topic across the top, section names and a coloured border

Plan

Short notes, not sentences. Put a ; between ideas to make dot points. The reasons or subtopics carry through to the body paragraphs.

Finish the piece

Type the parts that are left, or leave a box empty to skip it. Then it goes to read aloud and print.

Success criteria

Students see these as a numbered checklist. Put each step on its own line and keep it to two or three steps.

How a round works

Enter
Next step. Start writing, boards up, mark, write it up. The gold button does the same.
1to90
When marking, tick each group whose sentence meets the criteria. Every ticked group gets the same points.
★
When writing it up, tap the number of the group whose sentence you chose. They get a star, not extra points.
Esc
Step back or cancel typing
Space
Pause or restart the timer
T
Add 30 seconds
Q
Silence countdown. Tap the groups that are silent and ready to reward them
L
Show or hide the RQ, GS, SS labels in the piece
GA
Show the groups, adjust scores by hand
MSF
Music, sound effects, full screen
Tap a sentence
Fix a sentence already on the board. The timer pauses while you edit.
